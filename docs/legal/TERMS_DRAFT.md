# Terms of Use — DRAFT for legal review (OD-12)

> **Not legal advice and not ready to publish.** Points the final terms should cover, based on how the product
> works. Your lawyer writes the published version.

1. **Service:** a billing, stock and udhaar app for shops, with an operations team that may help set up items.
2. **Accounts:** each person signs in with their own phone or email; the shop owner controls who is a member and
   is responsible for staff actions in the shop.
3. **Shop data belongs to the shop.** We store and process it to provide the service; the owner can export it.
4. **Correctness:** the app calculates totals and tax as configured by the shop; the shop is responsible for its tax
   settings, prices and filings. Offline bills are sent when the phone is online; bills flagged for review need the
   owner's check.
5. **Plans and payment:** free trial, then the plan the shop chooses; limits per plan; when a plan ends the shop
   keeps billing on the free plan's limits; how payment and refunds work [to be decided, OD-05].
6. **Acceptable use:** no unlawful goods, no messages to customers without their consent, no attempts to access
   other shops' data or overload the service (rate limits apply).
7. **Pilot:** the pilot version may change and may have bugs; keep your own records during the pilot.
8. **Liability, suspension, termination, governing law and jurisdiction, grievance contact** [to be written].
