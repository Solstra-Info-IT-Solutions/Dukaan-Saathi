## What we collect, and why
| Data | From whom | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone number or email | Shop owner and staff | Sign-in (one-time code) | Supabase (India region, ap-south-1) |
| Name, shop name, address, GSTIN (optional) | Shop owner | Shop profile, bill header | Supabase |
| Items, prices, stock, bills, payments, expenses | Shop | The shop's own business records | Supabase and the shop's phones |
| Customer names and phone numbers, udhaar balances | Entered by the shop | The shop's credit book; reminders the shop sends on WhatsApp | Supabase and the shop's phones |
| Photos of item lists, expense receipts | Shop | Setting up items; expense proof | Private storage, shop-only access |
| Support messages | Shop | Answering questions | Supabase |
| Device id, app version | App | Bill numbering per phone, update notices | Supabase |
| Voice | Only while the mic button is held | Turning speech into item names on the phone's own speech service | **Not stored or sent to us**; only the resulting text, and words the owner teaches as item names |

## Our role
For customer data a shop enters (names, phone numbers, udhaar), the **shop** decides what to collect; we process it
only to provide the app to that shop.

## Sharing
No selling of data. Processors: Supabase (database, storage, sign-in), Google (Play services, speech on the phone),
SMS provider for sign-in codes [to be named], payment provider for plans [to be named]. Each shop's data is visible
only to its members and to our support staff when helping that shop (every access is logged).

## Keeping and deleting
Business records 8 years (tax law); logs 30 days; import working data 30 days; support attachments 1 year after a
ticket closes. A shop owner can ask for an export of their data or closure of the shop.

## Security
Encrypted connections; per-shop access rules in the database; staff access with two-step verification and an audit
log; phones keep data in the app's private storage.

## Your rights
Access, correction, erasure (subject to legal retention), grievance redressal, nominating a person — contact
[grievance officer].

## Changes
We publish a new version and ask every user to accept it in the app before continuing.
